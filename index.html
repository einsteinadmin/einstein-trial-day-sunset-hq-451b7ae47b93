Trial Day Sunset

Trial days are being retired — replaced by a culture interview, a mover-specific competency interview, a Human Potential Evaluation built with Concentra, and a 2-month Extended Trial Period with staged check-ins at weeks 1, 2, 4, and 8. The old process ran on an untrained crew's subjective gut call as a 1099-blurred field day, cost roughly $60K a quarter unbilled, and never actually tested for what the mover job requires. This page merges Anne's leadership-facing case for the change with Brian's step-by-step gap analysis into one shared reference — what's decided, what's still open, and what it actually takes to flip the switch.

Open questions, by owner

Resolved since Brian's HQ (no longer open): the clearance gate — a clear policy already exists (nothing employment-related happens until background, drug screen, and HPE all clear) and a working ClearCompany→Rippling mechanism already carries it (Matisen) · HPE branch coverage — protocol built and validated with Concentra since March, locations confirmed and equipped since June · competency-interview scoring + the culture interview — both already reviewed by Cameron, reworked, and programmed into ClearCompany by Jalissa (a doc-sync pass is still pending, not a structural gap) · check-in conversation deadlines — the mover app already has specific flags at weeks 1/2/4/8 outlining exactly what managers cover · termination authority when the BM is out — not a new gap, existing coverage structure applies · the coaching-redirect mechanism — already exists (a Monday board); if managers aren't logging redirects, that's a leadership/adoption issue, not a process gap.

Anne — process decisions

1
SLA socialization. Jalissa's Recruiting SLA Framework was published 7/30 but still hasn't been socialized to the 10 BMs. Target: start of next month.
2
Day-one presentation content. The 60-day eval walkthrough, the four measured criteria, and the CHOICES values walk don't exist as an actual presentation yet — a few hours of work, not a structural gap.
3
Mixed-signals framework — needs documenting, not deciding. The real judgment criteria already exist: has the manager held up their end (clear expectations, consistent check-ins, feedback on flagged struggle areas) vs. clear-cut employee signals (attendance issues, bad attitude, an unremorseful damage with no hustle to improve → cut). Just needs to be written down somewhere real.
4
In-flight candidates. Anyone mid-pipeline with a trial day already booked when this flips.
5
Is there actually a 100-point framework? The real instruments in use are CHOICES on a 5/3/1 tally, Get It / Want It / Can Do It, and +/− competency ratings — worth confirming this isn't getting conflated with the separate 100-Point Behavioral System rock (targeted Jan 1, 2027).
